Why Group Training Works

Real improvement happens with teammates, defenders, and decisions, not in isolation.

01

Teamwork & Communication

Group Training enhances teamwork and communication, crucial skills for any soccer player.

02

Game Like Scenarios

03

Competitive Environment

04

Motivation & Support

Training with peers provides motivation and support, making the learning process more enjoyable.

The Curriculum

What You'll Work On

At Foundry Soccer Academy, we focus on real results. Parents can expect their children to develop critical in-game technical skills and improved soccer intelligence.

Game Ready Moves

Croqueta, v cuts, scissors, and inside out cuts to beat defenders in 1v1s.

First Touch Control

Keep possession and transition into attacking play.

1v1 Confidence

Take on defenders with purpose and creativity in real match scenarios.

Agility & Awareness

Reaction time and positioning with soccer specific spatial awareness.

Composure & Decision-Making

Better decisions during high stakes moments.
